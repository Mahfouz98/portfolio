What I do

I came to DevOps from the bottom of the stack.

Before pipelines and clusters I was a network engineer at Orange Egypt, working on Cisco and Juniper routing, GPON and IPTV head-end equipment in the data center. In 2023 I joined ITI's nine-month Cloud Architecture & DevOps scholarship, and since 2024 I've been running production platforms at Misr Technology Services.

That path shapes how I work. I care about the whole delivery path, from the commit to the cluster to the network underneath it, and I'd rather automate a task once than do it by hand twice.
